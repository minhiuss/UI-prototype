# Surface brief: TradeUp web prototype (web/)

Scope: `web/index.html` (landing, Persuade), `web/flow.html` (customer S2–S8, Operate), `web/dealer.html` (appraiser A1–A3, Operate). Mock data in the browser; no backend calls.

Audience and job: a used-car owner on a phone wants the monthly top-up for one VinFast model; an appraiser on a desktop sets the official price. Proof: the estimate itself, with comparables, source and date. Constraints: every figure illustrative and labelled; "chính thức" only after the appraiser confirms.

Unresolved: real imagery (current images are low-resolution concept crops), real prices and promotions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is the calculator. The visitor types their car into the page and the number appears on it; it refuses the "feature grid + sign-up" page.

OWN-WORLD: Night-navy fields (#071a36) against ice blue (#eef4ff) and white, one electric blue (#1464F4) for every action and result. Pill buttons, 22px-radius panels, translucent navy controls on dark fields, a diagonal navy/ice split behind the estimator, tight heavy sans headlines.

STORY: Understand "trade in, know the monthly top-up" in one glance; believe it because each amount shows its basis and a person sets the final price; start the estimate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed navy hero with the VF 7 night photo right, headline "Đổi xe cũ. Lên đời VinFast." at clamp(44px,6vw,76px) left, two pill actions under it, a translucent "Khoản bù dự kiến / tháng" card floating right, then the four-step strip.
